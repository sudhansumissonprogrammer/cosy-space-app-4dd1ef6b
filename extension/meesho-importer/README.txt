SHOPBANAO MEESHO COLLECTOR

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, or Opera.
3. Turn on Developer mode.
4. Choose Load unpacked and select this folder.
5. Open a Meesho product link normally.
6. Open the extension and choose Collect this product.
7. Repeat for every product, then choose Export for ShopBanao.
8. In ShopBanao, open Products, choose Meesho Import, and upload the JSON file.

The extension removes tracking parameters. It never stores your ShopBanao password or private keys.
