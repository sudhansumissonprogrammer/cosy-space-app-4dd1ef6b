About Prodoxy

Entrepreneurs To Build & Grow Online

Prodoxy is an affordable all-in-one eCommerce platform built to help individuals and businesses launch professional online stores at just ₹99/month — without technical knowledge.

Our Story

Prodoxy was created with a simple belief — online selling should be affordable and accessible to everyone. Many small business owners struggle with high platform costs and complex setup processes. We built Prodoxy to remove those barriers.

Our goal is to simplify eCommerce for Indian entrepreneurs and provide powerful tools at the lowest possible price.

Our Mission

To make eCommerce accessible to every entrepreneur in India by providing powerful AI tools, simple store setup, and affordable pricing — so anyone can start selling online confidently.

Our Vision

To become India’s most trusted and affordable eCommerce ecosystem, enabling millions of sellers to build sustainable and profitable online businesses.

Meet The Founder

Prodoxy was founded by Shivam Singh, a self-made entrepreneur from Bihar who started his journey without financial support. After building multiple digital ventures and helping hundreds of sellers, he realized the need for an affordable eCommerce solution for India.

Our goal is simple — remove barriers and give every business owner the tools to succeed online.
