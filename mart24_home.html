Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your store.

Is Mart-24 really free to start?

Yes. There is zero setup cost to launch your online store on Mart-24. You only pay a 2% commission when you make a sale. If you don't sell, you don't pay anything — ever.

How does the 2% commission work?

Mart-24 charges a flat 2% commission on every successful sale. There are no monthly fees, no setup fees, and no hidden charges. The commission is automatically deducted from your payouts, so you never have to worry about it.

How does the AI store builder work?

After signing up, describe your brand, products, and preferred style to the AI agent in plain language. It builds a fully customized storefront in minutes — including layout, theme, and product pages. You can refine any detail through a natural chat interface using AI tokens.

Can I use my own custom domain?

Yes. Mart-24 supports custom domains at no extra charge. You can connect your existing domain or use a free mart-24.com subdomain (like yourstore.mart-24.com) when you're just getting started.

What payment methods does Mart-24 support?

Mart-24 supports Razorpay and PhonePe. You connect your own payment gateway account to your store — no lock-in, and setup takes just a few minutes.

Is Mart-24 good for Instagram and YouTube sellers?

Mart-24 is built specifically for social media sellers who want to convert their Instagram or YouTube audience into paying customers. Thousands of Indian creators with 10K–800K+ followers already use Mart-24 to run their online stores professionally.
