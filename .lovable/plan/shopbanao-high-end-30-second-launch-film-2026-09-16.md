# ShopBanao High-End 30-Second Launch Film

## Goal
Rebuild the promotional video from scratch so it feels like a premium product launch rather than a slideshow. The film will clearly show what ShopBanao does, why Indian D2C sellers need it, and how the whole business runs from one platform.

## Creative direction
- **Style:** editorial product cinema — deep teal/ink surfaces, ShopBanao red/blue/yellow accents, crisp real screens, controlled light, depth, and bold typography.
- **Typography:** a distinctive display face for large statements and one highly legible body face; no generic template styling.
- **Motion:** continuous camera-led movement, masked screen reveals, product-screen parallax, traced order paths, kinetic numbers, icon choreography, and seamless match transitions.
- **Sound:** a new clean cinematic rhythm bed with no sustained buzz or rising siren; professional narration remains dominant and intelligible throughout.

## 30-second story
1. **0–4s — Hook:** “Your business should not be scattered across ten tools.” Fragmented order, payment, WhatsApp and shipping elements converge into ShopBanao.
2. **4–9s — Build:** Real ShopBanao storefronts emerge from a fast catalogue-to-store transformation. “Launch a premium store built for your brand.”
3. **9–14s — Convert:** Real mobile checkout, UPI, COD, address fill and trust signals animate as one frictionless order journey.
4. **14–20s — Operate:** The order travels through WhatsApp, payment, inventory, logistics and notifications into one live command centre.
5. **20–25s — Scale:** AI Store Builder, AI calling, cart recovery, analytics and customer support appear as coordinated agents—not a grid of feature cards.
6. **25–30s — Resolve:** Multiple live brands form the ShopBanao identity. Close with “Everything you need to build and scale your business,” “₹0 subscription,” and “Flat 2% on every order.”

## Production work
- Re-audit the landing page and platform screens; use only accurate, supported claims.
- Use the main ShopBanao logo, strongest real storefront captures, real checkout/dashboard surfaces, payment and courier marks, and platform iconography.
- Recreate important UI as motion-ready layers where screenshots are too flat, while preserving the real product language.
- Build custom SVG paths, particles, depth layers, counters and transitions in Remotion; all movement remains frame-driven and deterministic.
- Write a new timed voiceover script paced for natural Indian-English delivery, with each spoken beat matched to visible proof.
- Create or source a fresh non-intrusive music bed and restrained transition sound effects; duck music beneath narration.

## Quality bar
- Story remains understandable with audio muted.
- Narration is clear at every second with no buzzer, siren, masking or dropout.
- No tiny unreadable feature tiles, long static holds, fake claims, generic stock visuals or repetitive transitions.
- Verify representative frames from every scene, full 1080×1920 playback, exact duration, audio loudness, safe text areas and final file integrity before delivery.
