# ShopBanao Cinematic SaaS Explainer

## Direction
Create a new 32–33 second vertical ShopBanao explainer inspired by the uploaded reference’s cinematic black space, cyan light, floating interface panels, contour pulses, and precise camera moves. It will be a distinct second film, not an edit of the previous video.

## Story
1. **Fragmented work** — orders, WhatsApp, shipping, checkout, and customer support appear as disconnected tools.
2. **One commerce system** — the fragments converge into ShopBanao.
3. **Build and sell** — real ShopBanao storefronts and product screens demonstrate polished stores and themes.
4. **Convert** — a compact checkout flow shows address autofill, UPI, COD, and payment options.
5. **Operate intelligently** — analytics, WhatsApp workflows, AI calling, inventory, and logistics form one connected command center.
6. **Scale without subscriptions** — close on ₹0 subscription and flat 2% per order, with the ShopBanao logo and website.

## Production
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, approximately 33 seconds.
- Use ShopBanao’s real logo, storefront screenshots, payment marks, and courier marks already available in the project.
- Use a premium geometric display face and highly legible body type.
- Build recurring motifs: cyan edge-light, expanding contour rings, floating glass interface panels, and light-tunnel transitions.
- Create a new concise Indian-English human voiceover and a restrained cinematic electronic soundtrack with clear narration throughout.
- Keep all claims accurate: ₹0 subscription, flat 2% per order, no ad-management claims.

## Quality checks
- Inspect key frames across every scene for clipping, empty compositions, and hierarchy.
- Verify the full exported MP4 decodes correctly at exactly the intended dimensions and duration.
- Measure narration loudness and ensure music never masks the voice.
