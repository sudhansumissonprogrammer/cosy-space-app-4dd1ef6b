## Phase 1 — Category page content (site work)
1. **Visible intro on every category page** (120–200 words, different for each page). It covers the styles, dial colours, strap types and price range actually in that category, plus COD and Rajkot dispatch. It is built from real product data, never invented specs.
2. **Heading matches search wording:** for example "First Copy Diesel Watches for Men" (now it shows only the category name).
3. **Short FAQ on each category page** (4–5 questions: price range, COD, delivery time, returns, genuine or replica). The answers use only facts already on the store.
4. **Live product count and price range** in the description, for example "120+ designs from ₹1,499".
5. **Related-category links** at the bottom (Diesel Men ↔ Diesel Women ↔ Fossil Men…) and a "Popular brands" link block on the homepage, so Google sees which categories matter.
6. **Product photo alt text** follows the category, for example "Diesel style black chronograph watch for men".

## Phase 2 — Clean up the catalogue structure
7. Find duplicate or overlapping categories (for example "casio-new" vs "casio") and merge or redirect them, so one strong page doesn't split its ranking with another.
8. Check that every category with demand exists (Fossil, Rolex, Cartier, AP) and has enough products. Thin categories stay out of search until they have more.
9. Add page-2+ links and check that each category's own sitemap lists every product.

## Phase 3 — Trust and outside signals
10. Show real reviews on category pages (a review strip using actual reviews from that brand's products).
11. Check the Rajkot store address and contact details on every page, and the Google Business Profile.
12. Track progress through Search Console, page by page.

## What you need to do (only you can)
1. **Publish the site and deploy the Worker.** Nothing above reaches Google fully until you do.
2. **Connect Google Search Console** and submit the sitemap. Without it we are working blind.
3. **Create a Google Business Profile** for the Rajkot store and collect reviews there.
4. **YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels** for each brand category ("First copy Diesel watch unboxing"), with links to the category page. These platforms already rank on page 1 for these searches.
5. **Real buyer reviews with photos.** Send a WhatsApp review request after delivery.
6. **Get mentions and links** from watch bloggers, Instagram reviewers and forums. Never buy links.
7. **Be patient.** Expect 4–12 weeks after Google re-crawls. No position can be guaranteed.

## What we will not do
Hidden text, keyword stuffing, fake reviews, copies of the same city or brand page, or claims that the watches are genuine or authorised by the brand. These get sites penalised and cancel out every gain above.

## Technical notes
- Category intro, FAQ and related links render in both the app and the search-engine HTML from one shared helper (same pattern as storeLocations), so both versions say the same thing.
- FAQPage schema only where the FAQ is visible on the page; BreadcrumbList and ItemList stay as they are.
- Data comes from existing products (price min/max, count, dial colour and strap from titles). Missing facts are left out.
- Tests: intro uses only real values, FAQ schema matches the visible FAQ, related links point to real public categories.
