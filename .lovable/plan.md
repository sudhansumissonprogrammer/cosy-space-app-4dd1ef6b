## Creative direction
- Masculine editorial watch campaign: black, silver, warm champagne, and restrained bronze.
- One premium modern sans-serif plus one elegant serif accent; mobile-safe kinetic typography throughout.
- Controlled push-ins, match cuts, masked text, subtle light sweeps, and faster detail cuts—no morphing, fake product imagery, glitch effects, or excessive transitions.
- Professional footage treatment: exposure and highlight recovery, balanced contrast, selective sharpening, stabilization only where useful, and natural skin tones.

## Voice and sound
- Natural Indian male English voice, approximately 25–35, confident and conversational—not overly deep, robotic, American, or TV-commercial-like.
- Use the supplied wording except for the unsupported trust line, which becomes: “Real product images, so you know what you’re ordering.”
- Minimal masculine fashion soundtrack with restrained bass and percussion, sidechain-ducked beneath narration; one subtle impact on ₹1,399.
